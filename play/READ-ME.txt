STARLIGHT REPAIR 0.1.38 — WEB REVIEW BUILD

Upload the entire contents of this folder to a static HTTPS host or an HTML game host.
index.html must be at the web root. Do not open the HTML directly with file://.
The Build/*.unityweb files use Unity's decompression fallback; keep their names unchanged.
Keep StreamingAssets/Cinematics/ and all three MP4 files. Serve MP4 as video/mp4;
HTTP byte-range support is recommended. New Game plays a clean 10-second opening.
Sector 7 completion plays the 15-second reopening; completing every sector plays
the 15-second Home at Last true ending. Simultaneous completion uses the true ending.
Esc, Space or Skip advances. Saved completed journeys do not replay their films.
For a host serving in an iframe, allow scripts, WebGL, fullscreen, audio and local storage.
Use ?lang=en to select English explicitly. Korean remains available from the menu or header.

Controls: WASD / arrows to fly, Space to brake, E to interact, F to wash, Esc for menu.
At the main station, open Craft tools: the wake nozzle costs 50 coins and the courier 100.
Away from a planet orbit, F toggles an owned nozzle to clear dust and gather loose scrap.
The courier collects full cargo, or half-full cargo after 30 seconds, and delivers it for you.
Use E near large containers to attach/release a tow; bring them to their S depot for 25 coins.
Stop near stranded robots in sectors 2–7 and press E to charge for 3 seconds, or wash them.
Clean every surface and repair every facility to start the habitat turbine and drifting lights.
Pirates scout for 9 seconds before approaching. Get within 4.5 units and press E to shoo them,
or use the original directional S-depot shield. Either successful defense earns 20 coins.
Menus: arrows or Tab / Shift+Tab, Enter or Space, Esc to go back.
Touch controls can be enabled in the page header. Landscape is recommended.

Start from Sector 1 for the full journey. Sector Select begins a fresh journey and asks
before replacing an existing save. Direct sector starts use matching equipment levels
(sector 1: level 1; sector 7: level 7). Saves retain their own equipment levels.
Cleaning earns 15 coins per surface, repairs 25, and each first sector restoration 50.
Pirates take 30% (minimum 5, maximum 60, never more than your balance); defense earns 20.
Saves belong to this browser and origin; clearing
site data or using private browsing can remove them. Web and Android saves are separate.

This package is prepared for review. It is not evidence of public hosting or submission.
